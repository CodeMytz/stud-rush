Stud Rush

Buy STUD with Sepolia ETH. Every pack is 100 STUD for 0.001 ETH. Three tiers, four options in each. Every option pays a bonus built with compound interest (salikti procenti): the pack grows by a rate for a number of rounds, then a flat fee is taken off. The cards show only the rate, the rounds and the fee. You work out which option pays the most.

The rate shrinks with every purchase, and after anyone buys, that option locks for every player, so the best answer changes while you calculate.

Ethereum Sepolia test network only. Test ETH has no value.

Math corner: salikti procenti

Smart picks: -

S = K · (1 + r)n

K is the start amount (100 STUD), r is the rate per round as a decimal (3% is 0.03), n is the number of rounds. Each round adds interest on the interest of the rounds before it.

Bonus = S − 100 − fee.
Example: 3.00% for 4 rounds with a 0.50 STUD fee.
S = 100 · 1.034 = 112.5509, so the bonus is 12.5509 − 0.50 = 12.0509 STUD. Adding 3% four times (12%) is simple interest and gives the wrong answer.

Rules

  • Price: 0.001 ETH buys one pack of 100 STUD plus the bonus of the option you pressed.
  • Bonus: compound interest. Bonus = 100 · (1 + rate)rounds − 100 − fee. The fee is a flat amount of STUD taken from the bonus (the bonus never goes below zero).
  • Tiers: Rush has the biggest rates and a 30 second lock, Steady a 60 second lock, Bargain bin the smallest rates and a 120 second lock. Each tier has four options with different rates, rounds and fees.
  • Shrinking rate: every purchase lowers that option's rate by a fixed step (down to its floor). Recompute with the new rate before you buy.
  • Recovery: every idle minute gives an option back 0.05% of rate, up to its starting rate.
  • Lock: after anyone buys, that option is locked for all players. The other options stay open.
  • GAME perk: holding at least 1 GAME adds a flat 1 STUD to every purchase.
  • Limit: 10 packs per wallet.
  • On-chain: price, compound bonus, locks, limits, stock and your results are checked and stored by the contract. This page only shows them and sends your transaction.

Stud Rush

5 rounds, 4 deals per round, 1 pick per round. Each deal grows 1000 points with compound interest (salikti procenti) and then a flat fee is taken off. The cards show only the rate, the periods and the fee, you do the maths. The contract scores every pick and the best deal of the round wins it. After round 5 the player with the most round wins gets 500 STUD.

value = 1000 × (1 + rate)periods − 1000 − fee

Round timers: 30 s, 45 s, 60 s, 75 s, 90 s. Entry 0.001 ETH (Sepolia test ETH, no value). Joining closes when round 2 starts. Tiebreak: higher total value, then ties on a single round go to the earlier pick.

Rules

  • Price: 0.001 ETH buys one pack of 100 STUD plus the bonus of the button you pressed.
  • Bonus: Rush starts at 5.00% and loses 0.50% per purchase (floor 1.00%). Steady starts at 3.00% and loses 0.25% (floor 0.50%). Bargain bin starts at 1.00% and loses 0.10% (floor 0.10%).
  • Recovery: every idle minute gives a button back 0.10%, up to its starting bonus.
  • Lock: after anyone buys, that button is locked for all players: Rush 30 seconds, Steady 60 seconds, Bargain bin 120 seconds.
  • GAME perk: holding at least 1 GAME adds 1.00% to every purchase.
  • Limit: 10 packs per wallet.
  • On-chain: price, bonus, locks, limits, stock and your results are checked and stored by the contract. This page only shows them and sends your transaction.
